Dixit Companion is the perfect scorekeeping app for your Dixit game nights. No more crumpled score sheets — manage your games right from your phone.

✨ KEY FEATURES

• Real-time score tracking for 3 to 6 players
• Automatic point calculation following the official rules (storyteller, found, voted)
• Two end-game modes: target score or number of turns
• Score evolution graph at the end of each game
• Full history of all your games with per-player statistics
• Built-in game rules, accessible at any time
• Modern, smooth Material Design 3 interface

📊 STATISTICS

Track your long-term performance: number of games played, wins, and much more. Compare your results with your friends and find out who the best storyteller at the table is.

🎮 HOW IT WORKS

1. Select your players
2. Choose the game objective
3. Play turn by turn — the app handles all the calculations
4. Check the final standings and statistics

Compatible with Dixit, Dixit Odyssey, Dixit Journey, and all expansions.

No ads. No internet connection required to play. Your data stays on your device.
